# RoomSense — 3-minute demo video

**Hard cap 3:00. Sound-on. Judges watch this live in the final round.**

## Before recording (Nov 1–2)
- Quiet hours; phone docked where it will actually sit; 3 rooms within earshot.
- Record 2–3 takes of every sound **from the actual rooms**; keep the best.
- Every number on screen must come from a real `/api/detect` response.
- Warm the Muse Spark cache by rehearsing the exact flow once.
