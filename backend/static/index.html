How it's built

iOS RoomPlan geometry → iPhone plays a 4 s log sweep and records the response at 3 positions → backend deconvolves to RIRs, computes ISO 3382 parameters, inverts per-surface absorption (Eyring + material priors), plans treatment, then simulates and renders the before/after.
